Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Monasa handles, why, and what you can do about it. It covers two different groups: the people we contact on behalf of our clients, and the people who visit this website or email us.

For work we do on behalf of a client, that client is the controller and Monasa acts as processor on their documented instructions. Where we build and maintain our own prospect research, we act as controller. In practice both roles are covered by the same protections described here.

If we contacted you

The short version

We contact people in their professional capacity, at a work address or work phone number, about something relevant to their job. We do not use home addresses or personal accounts. If you would rather not hear from us, reply to the email or tell the caller, and we stop and keep a record so it does not happen again. You can also email info@monasa.io at any time.

What we hold

For business contacts we typically hold:

  • Name and job title
  • Work email address and, where relevant, a work phone number
  • Employer, and publicly available information about that employer
  • The professional signal that made us think you were relevant, such as a job posting, a funding announcement, a public technology change, or a new office
  • A record of our contact with you, including messages sent, replies, and call notes or recordings where a call took place
  • The source each contact detail came from, and the date we obtained it

Where we get it

From publicly accessible professional sources, including company websites, public job listings, public business registers, press and news coverage, and professional networking profiles. We also use business contact data providers. We do not buy consumer data, and we do not scrape private accounts or content behind a login that is not ours.

Why we are allowed to

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our interest, and our client's, in reaching businesses that may want what they sell, balanced against your interest in not being bothered. We keep that balance by contacting people only in a professional role, only about something plausibly relevant to their work, and by stopping immediately when asked.

For telephone contact in Belgium we check the Bel me niet meer do-not-call list before calling. Where a country requires prior consent for a particular channel, we follow that rule instead of relying on legitimate interests.

You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing. If you object, we stop, and nothing needs to be weighed against it.

How long we keep it

Prospect data is kept while it is relevant to an active or planned campaign, and reviewed at least once a year. Records of an objection or opt-out are kept indefinitely, because that is the only reliable way to make sure we do not contact you again. Call recordings are kept for up to 12 months unless a client contract requires a shorter period.

If you visit this website

This site is deliberately simple. It sets no cookies, runs no advertising trackers, and has no analytics. There is no contact form, so nothing you type is collected here.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard server request logs including IP addresses for security and abuse prevention. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google receives your IP address as part of it. If you would rather avoid that, most browsers and content blockers can block external font loading without breaking the page.

If you email us

We keep your message and contact details so we can reply and, where it leads to a business relationship, to manage that relationship. Enquiries that do not lead anywhere are deleted within 24 months.

Who else sees the data

We use a small number of service providers to run outreach and store records. All are contracted as processors under Article 28 GDPR, and all data we handle is stored on servers within the European Economic Area. We do not transfer personal data outside the EEA.

The categories of provider we rely on:

  • Email sending and inbox infrastructure
  • Data enrichment and list building
  • Telephony and call recording
  • CRM and record keeping
  • Cloud hosting and storage

Where we act as processor for a client, data is also shared with that client, since the campaign is run for them. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's independent marketing.

If you want to know which specific providers hold data relating to you, ask at info@monasa.io and we will tell you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete your data, subject to the opt-out record noted above
  • Stop contacting you, at any time, for any reason or none
  • Restrict how we use your data while a dispute is resolved
  • Receive data you gave us in a portable format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests

Email info@monasa.io. We respond within 30 days and there is no charge. We may ask you to confirm your identity, but only where we genuinely cannot otherwise tell that the request is yours.

Security

Access to prospect and client data is limited to the people who need it. Accounts require multi factor authentication, data is encrypted in transit, and access is removed when someone stops working on an engagement. If a breach occurs that is likely to put people at risk, we notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and affected individuals without undue delay.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, tell us first at info@monasa.io and we will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority. In Belgium that is the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit, Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top. Material changes to how we handle prospect data will appear here before they take effect.
